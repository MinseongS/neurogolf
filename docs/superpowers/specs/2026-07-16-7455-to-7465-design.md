# NeuroGolf 7455 to 7465 Score Campaign Design

## Objective

Raise the current working manifest snapshot from 7455.0891 to at least 7465.10 through new, gated score improvements. Recovery of the already-earned 7455 board does not count toward the target.

The campaign optimizes for local score first under the repository's default 8000-overfit doctrine. Every adoption must still pass bundled fail=0 and be cheaper than the deployed incumbent through `ng gate` and `ng adopt`. Fresh evaluation remains diagnostic, while known Kaggle-zero and package-runtime failure modes remain explicit risk signals.

## Strategy

Run three lanes in order of expected return.

### 1. Global outlier search

Rebuild a current 400-task inventory from the deployed artifacts, not stale queue estimates. Rank tasks by the maximum removable counted carrier or parameter bank, converted to point gain. Prioritize mechanisms capable of at least +0.5 on one task:

- counted spatial carrier elimination through FREE input/output;
- final renderer absorption into Einsum, Conv, QLinearConv, or Gather-family output;
- repeated selector, coordinate, or polynomial-bank factorization;
- exact semantic collapse of a high-cost imported network;
- reusable mechanisms already recorded in `state/insights.yaml` but not rescanned against the current endpoints.

The desired result is one +3 to +7 outlier or several +0.5 to +2 wins. A queue row without a current byte-level saving proof is not treated as expected score.

### 2. Public teacher frontier

Search only newly available or not-yet-mined public artifacts. Public models are teachers: any min-merge win must be paired with source/operation autopsy and a full-board rescan for the recovered mechanism. Blind wholesale imports are excluded.

This lane is accepted only when it supplies either a directly gated cheaper artifact or a reusable lowering with concrete rescan targets.

### 3. Medium-yield harvest

Work current queue candidates whose measured upper bound is at least +0.1, beginning with the largest byte savings. This lane supplies the remainder after an outlier is found; it is not expected to produce all +10 alone.

## Execution Flow

1. Freeze a read-only inventory snapshot of the current 7455.09 deployment and manifest hashes.
2. Recompute actual cost, dominant tensors, parameter banks, existing source, task ledger history, and known reopen triggers for all 400 tasks.
3. Produce a ranked campaign queue containing predicted cost, predicted point gain, mechanism, evidence, runtime risk, and hidden-zero risk.
4. Deep-dive only the highest-ROI unblocked targets. Candidates remain under `candidates/taskNNN/`.
5. Verify each candidate in a fresh process, then run the mandatory `ng gate` and `ng adopt` path.
6. Register reusable insights and immediately rescan all 400 deployed models.
7. Stop when adopted gains exceed +10 with a small buffer, then perform a quiescent full-board verification before pack/submit.

## Risk Controls

- Preserve all unrelated dirty files and concurrent-session work.
- Never edit `submission/overfit_nets/` except through `ng adopt`.
- Do not repeat a ledger-negative family unless its recorded reopen trigger has fired.
- Profile variadic Einsum and other contraction-heavy candidates in isolated processes before full gating.
- Track task118, task131, and task209 public-zero repairs as protected invariants.
- Treat task-local learned bundled selectors as high-risk and require a larger gain threshold than semantic rewrites.
- Before submission, verify that no concurrent session has submitted an overlapping board.

## Success Criteria

The campaign succeeds when all of the following are true:

- working manifest is at least 7465.10;
- every included improvement was adopted through the normal gate;
- deployed and manifest hashes agree;
- a quiescent 400/400 verification completes without failures or uncaught timeouts;
- the protected public-zero repairs remain present;
- the resulting board can be reproduced from source-owned candidates or explicitly documented teacher-derived artifacts.

## Stop and Pivot Rules

- If the refreshed queue contains less than +4 of evidence-backed medium-yield work, do not grind micro-wins first; concentrate on outlier and teacher lanes.
- If an outlier family fails its bounded search, record the exact grammar/tool/date/reopen trigger and move to the next family.
- If adopted gains reach +10 but full verification exposes runtime interaction, remove the highest runtime-risk additions first rather than weakening correctness gates.
